# Common wire connects

## Bridge positive rails

`P --> P` chooses opposite positive rails and makes the wire red by default.

## Cross the centre gap

`R2C --> R2C` chooses a left and right terminal hole on row 2.

## Connect a rail to a terminal

`LP --> LT-R4C` chooses a nearby free left-positive rail hole and terminal column.
