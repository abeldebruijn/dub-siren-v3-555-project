# Positions and shortcuts

The board has a left positive rail, left ground rail, left terminal, right terminal, right positive rail, and right ground rail.

## Exact rail positions

| Form | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `LP1` | Left positive rail, row 1 |
| `LG1` | Left ground rail, row 1 |
| `RP1` | Right positive rail, row 1 |
| `RG1` | Right ground rail, row 1 |

Each rail is one continuous electrical group: `RP1` and `RP2` are connected. Positive and ground rails are separate: `RP1` and `RG1` are not connected.

## Exact terminal positions

`LT-R2C4` means left terminal, row 2, column 4. `RT-R2C4` is its right-terminal counterpart. All terminal holes in one side and row are internally connected. Across the centre gap, left and right remain separate.

For five columns, the physical order is:

```text
LT  5 4 3 2 1  |  1 2 3 4 5  RT
```

## Shortcuts

Shortcuts select the closest available endpoint pair. Occupied holes are skipped.

| Shortcut | Resolution idea |
| --- | --- |
| `LP` | First suitable row on the left positive rail |
| `P` | A suitable positive rail; side is inferred from the other endpoint |
| `R2C` | A suitable side and free terminal column on row 2 |
| `LT-R2C` | First suitable free column in left-terminal row 2 |
Examples include `P --> P` from the left positive rail to the right positive rail, and `R2C --> R2C` from the left terminal to the right terminal.
