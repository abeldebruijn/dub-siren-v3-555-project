# Colours

Components and wires accept named colours or six-digit hexadecimal colours.

## Named colours

`black`, `red`, `brown`, `orange`, `yellow`, `green`, `forest`, `blue`, `cyan`, `purple`, `grey`, and `white` are supported.

## Wire defaults

- A wire starting on a positive rail is red.
- A wire starting on a ground rail is black.
- Other starts choose deterministically from brown, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, grey, and white.
- Yellow and white receive a thin dark understroke for contrast.

## Saturation

`saturation 0%` removes all colour; `saturation 100%` preserves the original. Saturation changes colour intensity without making the wire transparent.
