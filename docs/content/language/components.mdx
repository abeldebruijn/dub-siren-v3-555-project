# Components and placement

V0.2 components are declared and placed once. All component names share one case-insensitive namespace. The old reusable `chip` plus separate `place` form is no longer valid.

## Singleton chip

```text
chip <name> {
  [height <integer>]
  [width <integer>]
  [color <colour>]
  pin <number> <alias> [| <alias> ...]
} at R<row> [flip]
```

`at R<row>` is required. Width defaults to `2`. Height may be explicit or inferred from an even highest pin number. Pins may be sparse. `flip` mirrors the physical footprint without changing numbers or aliases.

## Other component forms

- `led`, `capacitor`, and `resistor` use `from <endpoint> to <endpoint>`.
- `button`, `potentiometer`, and `switch` use `at R<row>` with optional placement modifiers.
- `annotation <number> at <endpoint>` adds a non-electrical numbered marker.

All placements reserve their physical leg holes. Chip bodies reserve covered terminal holes. Control bodies are visual and reserve only their legs. Overlap, board overflow, unavailable endpoints, and duplicate names are errors.

## References

Use a component name followed by a numeric pin or named terminal:

```text
wire NE555.3 --> D1.1
wire NE555.OUT --> S1.left1
annotation 1 at P1.wiper
```

Component references resolve to a free hole in the terminal's electrical group; they do not reuse the occupied leg hole.
