# Blink cycle timing

The learner changed `LED.blink()` to `on_time=0.1`, `off_time=0.1`, and `n=10`, then observed ten flashes. They understand that this accumulates to 1 second on plus 1 second off; one full cycle is 0.2 seconds, so the complete sequence lasts 2 seconds.

## Evidence

Reported the ten-blink result and correctly totalled the separate on-time and off-time.
