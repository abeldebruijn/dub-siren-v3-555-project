# Two selector bits reach three controls

Lesson 12 moves Rate from direct GP26 to CD74HC4051 CH2 pin 15 and moves S1 pin 10 from GND to GP19. The learner practices reading channel numbers as binary selector values: CH0 = 00, CH1 = 01 and CH2 = 10. Pitch, Amount and Rate can now share GP28 while GP26 becomes available for a later control.
