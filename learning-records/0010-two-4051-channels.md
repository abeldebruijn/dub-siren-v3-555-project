# Two 4051 channels selected by one Pico output

The learner stabilized the single-channel 4051 circuit after reseating loose breadboard connections. Lesson 10 moves Amount from direct GP27 ADC input to 4051 CH1, keeps Pitch on CH0, and uses GP18 to drive S0 while S1 and S2 stay grounded. Future mux lessons can add S1 and more channels after this two-channel selection is confirmed.
