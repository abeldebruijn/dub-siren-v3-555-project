Keep Mod Rate on GP26. Add a second B10K potentiometer on GP27 to control how bright each flash becomes. One knob controls *how fast*; the other controls *how much*. These become Mod Rate and Mod Amount on the final siren.

## 1. Name the existing knob

Your GP26 potentiometer is now **Mod Rate**. Keep its three wires unchanged. The new potentiometer is **Mod Amount**.

## 2. Add Mod Amount with USB unplugged

1. Connect one outside pin of the new B10K pot to the same `3V3(OUT)` supply.
2. Connect its centre wiper to `GP27 / ADC1`.
3. Connect its other outside pin to the same `GND`.
4. Leave GP26, GP14, GP15, and the LED resistor unchanged.
5. Bridge the left and right power rails: one wire from `LP` to `RP`, another from `LG` to `RG`.

<LessonFiveCircuit />

Most breadboards split their power rails at the centre gap, so the left half's rails are not electrically connected to the right half's rails even though they carry the same printed `+`/`−` labels. Mod Rate sits on the left and Mod Amount sits on the right, so without the `LP`→`RP` and `LG`→`RG` bridge, Mod Amount's rail pins would float and never actually reach 3V3 or GND.

:::danger Before reconnecting USB
Trace both pots separately. Each must read: 3V3 → resistance → GND, with only the centre pin going to an ADC. Never connect VBUS or VSYS to GP26/GP27. Also confirm both rail bridges are in place, or verify with a multimeter that your board's rails are already continuous.
:::

## 4. Prove each knob has one job

1. Hold Trigger. Set Amount halfway.
2. Turn Rate only: speed changes; peak brightness should not.
3. Set Rate near the middle.
4. Turn Amount only: brightness changes; speed should not.
5. Release Trigger: LED stops immediately.

Before continuing, predict the symptom if the two centre-wiper connections were exchanged.

:::details One knob works backwards
Unplug USB and exchange only that pot’s two outside wires. Do not move its centre wiper.
:::

:::details Amount at zero makes the flashing disappear
Correct: zero amount produces zero brightness. Raise Amount before diagnosing Rate.
:::
