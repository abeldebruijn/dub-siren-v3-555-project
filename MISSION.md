# Mission: Build a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 dub siren

## Why
Learn practical electronics and Pico programming by building a playable Dub Siren V3. Each small circuit should become part of the final instrument rather than an unrelated exercise.

## Success looks like
- Safely build and troubleshoot 3.3 V breadboard circuits
- Blink and replace standard two-leg LEDs
- Read buttons, potentiometers, and the five-position selector
- Turn the purchased controls into a USB-MIDI dub-siren controller for a Mac
- Later add standalone audio after buying and validating a PCM5102A module

## Constraints
- Start with no assumed Pico or electronics experience
- Use the parts marked bought in `shopping-raspberry.md`
- Use the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 with headers
- Use MicroPython and `picozero` for beginner control lessons
- Reassess the runtime when class-compliant USB-MIDI begins
- Keep early prototypes solder-free
- Reserve GP10, GP11, and GP12 for a possible future I²S DAC

## Out of scope
- Mains voltage or other hazardous power work
- The older Raspberry Pi Zero and analogue 555 build routes
- Standalone audio until the optional DAC is purchased
