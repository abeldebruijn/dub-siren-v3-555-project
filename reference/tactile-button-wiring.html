Dub Siren V3 · quick reference

Inside a four-leg button

Four metal legs, but only two electrical sides. Pressing joins those sides.

Lesson 2 Pico wiring picozero buttons

Released

one connected side
open
other connected side

The two legs on one side are already connected. The two legs on the opposite side are also connected. Between the sides, the switch is open.

Pressed

GP14 → button
side A pressed → button
side B → GND

Pressing bridges the two sides. The button has no positive or negative direction.

Breadboard rule

  1. Unplug USB.
  2. Place the button across the breadboard’s centre trench.
  3. Connect GP14 to one side.
  4. Connect GND to the opposite side.
If it acts permanently pressed Your two wires probably use legs from the same internally connected side. Unplug USB, move one wire to the opposite side, then retry.
Button shapes vary. If the moulding makes the pairs unclear, use a multimeter’s continuity mode while the button is disconnected.
